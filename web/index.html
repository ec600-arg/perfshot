FAQ

Common questions

What devices does it work on?
Any modern smartphone or tablet with a browser — iOS Safari, Android Chrome, or any other browser. No app store download needed. You can add it to your home screen for a native app experience.
Do I need internet during training?
Only to verify your subscription (once per 24 hours). After that the app works completely offline, which is important on ranges without good signal.
Which smart bulbs are supported?
LIFX bulbs (direct WiFi, no hub needed) and Govee RGB bulbs. The lamp feature is optional — the app works perfectly without one.
Can I use it on multiple devices?
Yes. Your subscription is tied to your email. You can open the app on any device and enter your email to access it.
Is it compliant with ISSF rules?
The timing follows ISSF 25m Sport Pistol specifications: 4-minute fire window for Precision, 3s target exposure / 7s interval for Rapid-Fire, 7-second attention delay, 1-minute loading time per series.
